Sera hii inaeleza jinsi HelaBox (programu ya simu ya Android na dashibodi ya mtandaoni) inavyokusanya, kutumia, kuhifadhi na kulinda taarifa binafsi za watumiaji na wanachama wa vikundi vya kuweka akiba na kukopa (VSLA). Kwa kutumia HelaBox unakubali sera hii.

1. Sisi ni nani

HelaBox inaendeshwa na [JINA KAMILI LA KISHERIA LA MMILIKI], [ANWANI], Tanzania ("sisi"). Mfumo umetengenezwa na kuhudumiwa kiufundi na Dephics kwa niaba yetu. Kwa maswali kuhusu faragha wasiliana nasi kupitia [BARUA PEPE].

2. Taarifa tunazokusanya

  • Taarifa za akaunti: jina, namba ya simu, jukumu lako (k.m. kiongozi wa kikundi, afisa, msimamizi) na nafasi yako katika kikundi.
  • Taarifa za wanachama: zinazoingizwa na viongozi wa kikundi — jina, namba ya simu, jinsia, namba ya uanachama, tarehe ya kujiunga na hali ya uanachama.
  • Taarifa za kikundi: jina la kikundi, mkoa, wilaya, kata, kijiji, idadi ya wanachama na kanuni za kikundi.
  • Kumbukumbu za fedha: michango, hisa, mfuko wa jamii, mikopo na marejesho, faini, matoleo, matumizi ya kikundi na mikopo ya serikali kwa kikundi.
  • Kumbukumbu za mikutano: tarehe, mahali (maandishi), mahudhurio na maelezo ya mkutano.
  • Taarifa za kuingia na usalama: namba za uthibitisho (OTP) za muda mfupi, tokeni za kuingia, kitambulisho cha kifaa na aina ya kivinjari/programu, na kumbukumbu ya ukaguzi (nani alifanya nini na lini).
  • Kumbukumbu za SMS: ujumbe uliotumwa, namba ya simu iliyopokea na hali ya utumaji.

Hatukusanyi mahali ulipo (GPS), anwani za simu (contacts), picha, kamera wala kipaza sauti. Hatuonyeshi matangazo na hatuuzi taarifa zako.

3. Jinsi tunavyotumia taarifa

  • Kuendesha huduma: kurekodi na kuonyesha akiba, hisa, mikopo, faini na mikutano ya kikundi.
  • Kukuthibitisha unapoingia kwa kutuma namba ya OTP kwa SMS.
  • Kutuma SMS za uthibitisho wa miamala, vikumbusho na matangazo ya kikundi kwa wanachama.
  • Kutoa ripoti kwa kikundi, wabia na wasimamizi wa programu kulingana na ruhusa zao.
  • Kulinda usalama wa mfumo, kuzuia udanganyifu na kuweka kumbukumbu ya ukaguzi.
  • Kutoa msaada wa kiufundi na kuboresha huduma.

4. Nani anaona taarifa

Ufikiaji unadhibitiwa kwa majukumu. Viongozi na maafisa wa kikundi wanaona kikundi chao tu. Wasimamizi wa klasta na watumiaji wa wabia wanaona vikundi walivyopangiwa tu. Wasimamizi wa mfumo wanaona taarifa zote kwa ajili ya kuendesha na kusaidia huduma.

5. Kushirikisha taarifa

Hatuuzi wala kukodisha taarifa binafsi. Tunashirikisha taarifa pale tu inapohitajika:

  • Mtoa huduma wa SMS (Beem Africa) — hupokea namba ya simu na maandishi ya ujumbe ili kuutuma.
  • Mtoa huduma wa seva (hosting) — anahifadhi mfumo na hifadhidata kwa niaba yetu.
  • Wabia wa programu waliopangiwa vikundi husika — kwa ajili ya usimamizi na ripoti.
  • Mamlaka za kisheria — pale tunapolazimika kisheria.

6. Uhifadhi na usalama

Taarifa zinahifadhiwa kwenye seva tunazozisimamia. Tunatumia kuingia kwa OTP, vipindi vya kuingia vinavyofungwa kwenye kifaa, udhibiti wa ufikiaji kwa majukumu na kumbukumbu ya ukaguzi ya kila badiliko. Hakuna mfumo ulio salama kwa asilimia 100, lakini tunachukua hatua stahiki kulinda taarifa zako.

7. Muda wa kuhifadhi

  • Kumbukumbu za fedha hazifutwi: makosa hurekebishwa kwa kugeuza muamala ili historia ya kikundi ibaki kamili. Zinahifadhiwa muda wote kikundi kinapotumia HelaBox na kadri sheria inavyotaka.
  • Watumiaji na wanachama husitishwa (deactivated) badala ya kufutwa pale wanapokuwa na kumbukumbu za fedha.
  • Namba za OTP zinaisha muda baada ya dakika chache.
  • Kumbukumbu za ukaguzi na SMS zinahifadhiwa kwa ajili ya uwajibikaji.

8. Haki zako

Kwa mujibu wa Sheria ya Ulinzi wa Taarifa Binafsi ya Tanzania (2022), una haki ya kuomba kuona taarifa zako, kuzirekebisha, kuzifuta (pale inaporuhusiwa), na kupinga matumizi fulani. Wanachama wanaweza pia kumwomba kiongozi wa kikundi chao kurekebisha taarifa zao. Tuandikie kupitia [BARUA PEPE]; tutajibu ndani ya siku 30.

9. Kufuta akaunti

Ili kufuta akaunti yako ya HelaBox, tuma ombi kutoka namba ya simu iliyosajiliwa kwenda [BARUA PEPE] au [NAMBA YA SIMU / WHATSAPP], ukitaja jina lako na kikundi chako. Tutasitisha akaunti yako na kuondoa taarifa zako binafsi ambazo hazihitajiki tena ndani ya siku 30. Kumbukumbu za fedha za kikundi zinabaki kwa kuwa ni mali ya kikundi na zinahitajika kwa hesabu za wanachama wengine.

10. Watoto

This policy explains how HelaBox (the Android app and the web dashboard) collects, uses, stores and protects the personal information of users and members of village savings and loan associations (VSLAs). By using HelaBox you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

HelaBox is operated by [OPERATOR FULL LEGAL NAME], [ADDRESS], Tanzania ("we", "us"). The system is built and technically maintained by Dephics on our behalf. For privacy questions contact us at [EMAIL].

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, phone number, your role (e.g. group leader, officer, administrator) and your position in a group.
  • Member information: entered by group leaders — name, phone number, gender, member number, joining date and membership status.
  • Group information: group name, region, district, ward, village, member counts and the group's rules.
  • Financial records: contributions, shares, social fund, loans and repayments, fines, withdrawals, group expenses and government loans to the group.
  • Meeting records: dates, location (as text), attendance and meeting notes.
  • Sign-in and security data: short-lived one-time codes (OTP), session tokens, a device identifier and browser/app type, and an audit log (who did what, and when).
  • SMS logs: messages sent, the receiving phone number and delivery status.

We do not collect your location (GPS), contacts, photos, camera or microphone. We show no advertising and we do not sell your information.

3. How we use information

  • To run the service: record and show a group's savings, shares, loans, fines and meetings.
  • To verify you when you sign in, by sending a one-time code by SMS.
  • To send members SMS confirmations of transactions, reminders and group announcements.
  • To produce reports for the group, partners and programme managers, according to their permissions.
  • To keep the system secure, prevent fraud and keep an audit trail.
  • To provide technical support and improve the service.

4. Who can see information

Access is controlled by role. Group leaders and officers see only their own group. Cluster managers and partner users see only the groups assigned to them. Platform administrators can see all data in order to operate and support the service.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only where needed:

  • SMS provider (Beem Africa) — receives the phone number and message text in order to deliver the message.
  • Hosting provider — stores the system and its database on our behalf.
  • Programme partners assigned to the relevant groups — for supervision and reporting.
  • Legal authorities — where we are required by law.

6. Storage and security

Data is stored on servers we control. We use OTP sign-in, sessions bound to the signed-in device, role-based access control and an audit log of every change. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

7. Retention

  • Financial records are never deleted: mistakes are corrected by reversing a transaction so the group's history stays complete. They are kept for as long as the group uses HelaBox and as long as the law requires.
  • Users and members who have financial records are deactivated rather than deleted.
  • One-time codes expire after a few minutes.
  • Audit and SMS logs are kept for accountability.

8. Your rights

Under Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act (2022) you may ask to access your information, correct it, delete it (where permitted) and object to certain uses. Members can also ask their group leader to correct their details. Write to us at [EMAIL]; we reply within 30 days.

9. Deleting your account

To delete your HelaBox account, send a request from your registered phone number to [EMAIL] or [PHONE / WHATSAPP], giving your name and your group. We will deactivate your account and remove personal information we no longer need within 30 days. The group's financial records are kept, because they belong to the group and are needed for other members' balances.

10. Children

HelaBox is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
